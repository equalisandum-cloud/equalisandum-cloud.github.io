## Get in touch

I am open to collaboration with researchers, public authorities, businesses and civil-society organizations working on cross-sectoral partnerships, particularly in Region Zealand. Contact me at [alq@ruc.dk](mailto:alq@ruc.dk).

## From followers to partners by design

My earlier work describes how firms behave once they are inside public-led partnerships. The next step is to ask how partnerships can be designed so that firms' interests and constraints are taken into account from the outset, rather than handled after the agenda has been set. This shifts the focus from what firms do as followers to how conveners structure participation, value capture and commitments.

In the longer term I hope to turn this line of work into a platform or service that gives Danish firms, nonprofits and public authorities more stable conditions for collaborating beyond single project cycles.

## Leaders who cannot be replaced

Many partnerships depend on one or two people who hold relationships, knowledge and legitimacy that others cannot easily take over. When they leave, the collaboration often weakens or ends. I plan to develop a theory paper on why such key members are hard to substitute and how their departure undermines durability.

## Firm–authority collaboration for resilience

I am exploring how firms and public authorities can collaborate to build resilience to hybrid threats, an area where private actors control much of the critical infrastructure but planning has traditionally been public.
